# FinNova Wallet: Security and Sign-in Policy

## Signing in
Sign in with your email and password. A second step follows: a one-time code (OTP) is sent to your registered mobile number, and you must enter it to finish signing in.

If you enter a wrong email or password, the app does not reveal which one was wrong. It shows how many attempts you have left.

## Login lockout
After 5 wrong password attempts the account is locked for 60 seconds. You can wait, or reset your password from the Forgot password page.

## One-time codes (OTP)
- Codes have 6 digits.
- A code expires after 5 minutes and can be used once.
- You get 3 attempts to enter the code. After 3 wrong attempts the code is locked and you must request a new one.
- You can ask for a new code after 30 seconds.

## Extra confirmation for larger payments
Payments of 50.00 or more need a fresh one-time code before they are sent. This is called step-up authentication, and it protects you if someone gets hold of an unlocked phone.

## Automatic sign-out
You are signed out after 3 minutes without any touch input. Sign in again to continue.

## Resetting a password
Choose Forgot password on the sign-in page, enter your email, confirm with a one-time code and choose a new password.

## Protecting your account
- FinNova never asks for your password or one-time code by phone, email or chat. Never share them.
- Freeze your virtual card on the Cards page if you think it is at risk.
- Check History regularly. Every transaction has a receipt and a reference.
- Account numbers include check digits, so a mistyped number is caught before money moves.
- Only scan payment QR codes you trust. Codes that are not FinNova payment codes are rejected.

## Reporting a problem
If you see a transaction you do not recognise, freeze your card, change your password and contact support through the details in the FAQ document.
