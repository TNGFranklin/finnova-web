# FinNova Wallet: Fees and Charges

All fees are shown on screen before you confirm a payment. Fees are charged on top of the amount, so the recipient or your balance receives the full amount.

## Free services
- Sending money to another FinNova wallet (peer-to-peer transfer): no fee.
- Paying by scanning a FinNova payment QR code: no fee.
- Moving money into or out of a savings pot: no fee.
- Opening an account, viewing balances and using the virtual card controls: no fee.
- Adding money with UPI (India): no fee.
- Sending a SEPA transfer to a euro bank account: no fee.

## Currency exchange between your own balances
- Fee: 0.5 % of the amount converted.
- Example: converting 100.00 EUR costs a 0.50 EUR fee, and the remaining 99.50 EUR is converted at the current rate.

## Bill payments
- Fee: 0.5 % of the bill, with a minimum of 0.10.
- The fee is added to the bill. Example: a 40.00 bill costs 0.20 in fees, so 40.20 leaves your wallet.
- Bill payments are checked against the monthly card limit.

## Worked example
Adding 100.00 EUR by card costs 2.9 % (2.90) plus 0.25, which is 3.15 EUR in fees. The card is charged 103.15 EUR and the balance is credited with the full 100.00 EUR.
