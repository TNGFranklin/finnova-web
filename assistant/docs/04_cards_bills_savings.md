# FinNova Wallet: Cards, Bills and Savings

## Virtual debit card
Every member has a virtual debit card. Card numbers are derived from the wallet ID. On the Cards page you can:
- Freeze and unfreeze the card at any time. A frozen card cannot be used until you unfreeze it.
- Set a monthly spending limit. The default monthly limit is 1,000.00.
- View the card details.

Card spending includes bill payments. A payment that would take you past the monthly limit is declined with a message showing your limit. Raise the limit on the Cards page if you need to spend more.

## Paying bills
On the Bills page choose a biller, enter the account or reference and the amount, and confirm.
- Fee: 0.5 % of the bill, but never less than 0.10 (whichever is higher, not both), added on top of the bill.
- The total (bill plus fee) is shown before you pay and must be available in your balance.
- The payment is checked against the monthly card limit.
- A bill can be repeated from History using the previous amount.

## Savings pots
A savings pot is a goal that holds money set aside from your wallet.
- Create a pot with a name and a goal amount.
- Add money to a pot, or take it back out to the wallet whenever you need it. Moving money in or out is free.
- Each pot shows progress towards its goal, and the Savings page shows the total saved across all pots.
- A pot is marked reached once the saved amount is at least the goal.
- Savings pots do not pay interest in FinNova Wallet.
