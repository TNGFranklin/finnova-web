# FinNova Wallet: Frequently Asked Questions and Support

## Where can I use FinNova Wallet?
- Website: https://tngfranklin.github.io/finnova-web/
- Android: download the FinNova APK from the same website.

## Is my money real?
No. FinNova Wallet is a demonstration and teaching project. Payment providers run in sandbox mode and no real money moves. The blockchain bank uses the Sepolia test network.

## Does FinNova offer loans, mortgages or overdrafts?
No. FinNova Wallet currently offers wallets in 11 currencies, top-ups, transfers, mobile money and remittances, bill payments, a virtual debit card and savings pots. Loans, mortgages and overdrafts are not offered.

## Is there a limit on how much I can send?
Payments of 50.00 or more need a one-time code. Card and bill payments are limited by the monthly card limit, which is 1,000.00 by default and can be changed on the Cards page. No other fixed limit is set for transfers between FinNova wallets, but your balance must cover the amount and any fee.

## Why is my payment declined?
Common reasons: the balance is too small to cover the amount plus the fee, the card is frozen, the monthly card limit would be exceeded, the one-time code was wrong or expired, or the account number failed its check digits.

## How do I change my password?
Use Forgot password on the sign-in page and confirm with the code sent to your mobile number.

## How do I find my account number?
Open Profile. The account number for each currency is shown there, in the form FN, two check digits, the currency code and ten digits.

## How do I get a receipt?
Open History, choose the transaction and download the receipt.

## What happens if I am inactive?
You are signed out after 3 minutes without touching the screen.

## Contact and support
FinNova Wallet is a student project. For questions, contact the project team through the repository at https://github.com/TNGFranklin/finnova-web. Support staff will never ask for your password, one-time code or seed phrase.
