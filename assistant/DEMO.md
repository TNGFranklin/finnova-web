## 2. How much does it cost to pay a 40 euro bill?

To pay a 40.00 EUR bill, you will be charged a fee of 0.5% of the bill, which is 0.20 EUR, plus the minimum fee of 0.10 EUR. 

So, the total fee is 0.20 EUR + 0.10 EUR = 0.30 EUR. 

The total amount you will pay is the bill plus the fee, which is 40.00 EUR + 0.30 EUR = 40.30 EUR.

## 5. How do I send money to an M-Pesa number, and what does it cost?

To send money to an M-Pesa number, follow these steps:

1. Open the Mobile money page in FinNova Wallet.
2. Choose the M-Pesa service.
3. Enter the recipient's M-Pesa mobile number.
4. Choose the currency to send from.
5. Enter the amount you want to send.
6. Check the fee, exchange rate, and what the recipient gets.
7. Confirm the transaction.

The fee for sending money to an M-Pesa number is 1.0% of the amount sent.

## 7. What are the common reasons a payment is declined?

The common reasons a payment is declined are:

1. The balance is too small to cover the amount plus the fee.
2. The card is frozen.
3. The monthly card limit would be exceeded.
4. The one-time code was wrong or expired.
5. The account number failed its check digits.
